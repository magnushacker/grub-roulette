How the pick gets chosen

First, some places get ruled out

  • Anywhere you or a joining colleague has blacklisted
  • Anywhere serving a cuisine you or a joining colleague marked ✗ don't suggest
  • Anywhere you've logged a visit to in the last {{ exclude_days }} days
  • Anything outside your search radius

Then everything left gets scored on:

  • Rating (45%) — see below for how this is built
  • Distance (25%) — closer beats farther, scaled across your search radius
  • Preferred cuisine (15%) — a bonus if it matches a cuisine you or a colleague marked ✓ prefer
  • Randomness (15%) — a small random nudge, so it's not the exact same answer every time

Where the rating comes from

In priority order, using the highest-confidence source available for that specific place:

  • If you or a joining colleague has rated this exact restaurant, that rating mostly decides it (blended a bit with its Google/Yelp rating)
  • Otherwise, if you or a colleague has rated other restaurants of the same cuisine, that cuisine track record counts for something (blended more heavily with Google/Yelp)
  • Otherwise, it just falls back to the average Google/Yelp rating

Why it's not always the top scorer

The highest-scoring places are most likely to get picked, but not guaranteed — it's a weighted draw among the top few, which is what keeps "Find lunch" feeling like a roulette spin instead of the same answer every day.
